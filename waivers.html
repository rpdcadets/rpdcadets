One more question

Does a second parent or guardian also need to sign?

A second signature is required on the Photo and Simunitions forms only when the cadet's parents are divorced or unmarried with shared rights. It is not required if one parent has sole legal custody, or for married parents or a single guardian.

Which Release of Liability applies?

The Release of Liability is signed on paper, and there are two versions for a minor cadet. Pick the one that fits the cadet's family so the advisors know which to expect.

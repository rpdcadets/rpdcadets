Support the Program

Donate

Your support funds competition travel, uniforms, training equipment, awards, and program scholarships for families in need. Every dollar helps keep the program within reach of every cadet.

Donations are received by the Richardson Citizen Police Academy Alumni Association (RCPAAA), a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, US Internal Revenue Tax ID 75-2484541. All donations to the RCPAAA are fully deductible to the extent provided by law.

Important

To make sure your gift reaches the cadets, designate "Public Safety Cadets" on your donation: in the Reference field for credit card gifts, or on the check memo line for gifts by mail. Undesignated credit card donations default to the RCPAAA's Back the Blue Fund.

Donate by Credit Card

Give securely online through the RCPAAA's donation portal. Enter "Public Safety Cadets" in the Reference field so your gift is directed to the cadet program.

Donate Now

Donate by Check

Make checks payable to the "Richardson Citizen Police Academy Alumni Association" and write "Public Safety Cadets" on the memo line. Print and enclose the Check Donor Form so your gift can be acknowledged for tax purposes. On the form, write "Public Safety Cadets" on the "Designated for" line, same as the check memo.

Mail your check and Check Donor Form to:
Richardson Citizen Police Academy Alumni Association
c/o Richardson Police Department
PO Box 831078
Richardson, TX 75083-1078
Attn: RCPAAA Treasurer

In-Kind Donations & Sponsorships

In-kind donations are welcome year-round: meals, water and sports drinks, printing, equipment, and awards. For in-kind gifts, business sponsorships, or questions about giving, email our advisors at advisors@rpdcadets.com.

Every sponsor is gratefully acknowledged. Sponsor names are recognized in program materials in accordance with program and city guidelines. Recognition is an acknowledgment of support and is not an endorsement of any business, product, or service by the City of Richardson or the Richardson Police Department.

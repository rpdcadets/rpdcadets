APOWW Form

You can use this form to prefill your APOWW and have the nursing staff print it out. Have them go to rpdcadets.com/patrol and enter your code; it will download for them to print.

You also have the option of downloading the PDF yourself and emailing it to the ER nurse directly.

Desktop code

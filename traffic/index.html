Traffic Stop Notice

Prints the English/Spanish feedback notice slip on the in-car printer, with more languages when you need them. ‹ All tools

Interim tool. Runs entirely in this browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere except to the printer.

Also print in

English and Spanish always print. Tap a language to add it to this slip; the choice clears when the page is reopened.

The printer does not cut between copies. Tap Print once per slip you need.

If a device list pops up, pick the one that says Serial Printer (for example Serial Printer (COM8)), not the printer serial number.

Printer setup and troubleshooting

First time on this computer (about 30 seconds)

  1. Turn the printer on. The MDC is already paired to it.
  2. Tap Print Notice. Chrome shows a small popup asking which device to use. That is a one-time permission, not a settings change.
  3. In the popup, scroll down to the entry that says Serial Printer followed by a COM number (for example Serial Printer (COM8); the number is different on each car). Pick it and tap Connect. Do not pick the entry that shows only the printer serial number; it looks right but does not deliver the job. The slip prints after a few seconds.

No "Serial Printer" entry in the list? Pair the printer first

  1. Note the serial number on the printer's bottom label (starts with XXRBJ).
  2. On the computer open Settings, then Bluetooth & devices, then Add device, then Bluetooth.
  3. Pick the entry that matches the printer serial number and tap Connect. A PIN pops up on the computer: press the checkmark button on the printer to accept it. When the computer says Connected, tap Done. Then come back here and do the three steps above.

After setup

  • Print Notice is one tap. Chrome remembers the printer on this computer.
  • Nothing prints? Make sure the printer is on and has paper, then try again.
  • Popup has no Serial Printer entry? The printer is off, or this MDC is not paired to it. See the pairing steps above.
  • Says "Sent to printer" but nothing comes out, or picked the wrong device? Tap Choose different printer below, then Print Notice again and pick the Serial Printer entry.
  • Printer replaced or swapped cars? Same thing: Choose different printer, then pair the new one.

Compatibility mode sends the slip uncompressed. Slower, but turn it on if slips stop printing on a particular printer.

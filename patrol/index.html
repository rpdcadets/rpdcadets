Tools

›
Traffic Stop / Citations
Use this on your squad car computer when someone has no scannable DL/ID. Type their information and it generates a barcode you can scan straight into your Brazos. Also has the Traffic Feedback Notice, which prints the English/Spanish feedback notice slip on your in-car printer.
 ›
Booking Slip / Medical Questionnaire
Fill this out on your work phone or squad computer, as much or as little as you want, then give the code to Detention staff to print the booking slip and medical screening. Tip: send the code over the MDC while you're enroute so it's ready when you arrive.
 ›
APOWW Form (DRAFT - DO NOT USE)
Prefill your APOWW on scene, then give the code to the ER nursing staff to print, or download the PDF and email it to them directly.
 ›
Emergency Protective Order Packet (DRAFT - DO NOT USE)
Every required EPO form in one place. Type the information once and it fills the matching fields on all of them, plus two extra forms the jail asked for to make their side easier.
 ›
Driver Information Exchange (DRAFT - DO NOT USE)
Crash driver exchange slip. Add as many units as were involved, type driver, vehicle, and insurance for each, and print one slip per driver on your in-car printer.
 ›
Electronic Vehicle Impound (DRAFT - DO NOT USE)
Vehicle impound and towed vehicle receipt. Type the vehicle, hold, owner, tow, and inventory details, then print the receipt on your in-car printer.

Desktop code

Got a code from an officer or MDC? Enter it below to pull up their Booking Slip, EPO Packet, or APOWW and print the filled forms on this computer.

Codes work once and expire 20 minutes after they are created.

    How the picture is drawn

    Containment ring: K9 containment doctrine, 2 to 3 blocks for every minute since the suspect was last seen (2.5 by default), using the block size measured from the streets around the pin. Likely hiding area: most urban foot suspects go to ground within about 4 blocks of where they were last seen (Calgary Police dog-section study). Direction wedge: about half of fleeing or lost subjects are found within 30 degrees of their last direction of travel and three quarters within 66 (search-and-rescue dispersion data). Vehicle reach: travel time over the road network at posted speeds from OpenRouteService; a fleeing driver can beat it. Spots: real intersections just outside the ring, corners first.

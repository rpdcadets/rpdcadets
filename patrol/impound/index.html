Printer setup and troubleshooting

First-time setup on this computer (about 2 minutes)

  1. Turn the printer on. Note the serial number on its bottom label (starts with XXRBJ).
  2. On the computer open Settings, then Bluetooth & devices, then Add device, then Bluetooth.
  3. Pick the entry that matches the printer serial number and tap Connect. A PIN pops up on the computer: press the checkmark button on the printer to accept it. When the computer says Connected, tap Done.
  4. Come back here and tap Print Receipt. Chrome shows a small popup asking which device to use. That is a one-time permission, not a settings change.
  5. In the popup, pick the entry with the printer serial number (it may also say "Standard Serial over Bluetooth link"), then tap Connect. The slip prints.

After setup

  • Print is one tap. Chrome remembers the printer on this computer.
  • Nothing prints? Make sure the printer is on and has paper, then try again.
  • Popup list empty? The printer is off or not paired. Repeat steps 1 to 3.
  • Picked the wrong device, or swapped cars? Tap Choose different printer, then Print again.
